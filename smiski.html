Smiski Adventures Website

For my Web Development class at WashU, I designed and built Smiski Adventures, an interactive digital dollhouse for product discovery. Pairing front-end design with Codex AI, I built a multi-room environment with seamless navigation, clickable character modals, and a sidebar to save favorite Smiskis across sessions. To make the world feel more immersive, real-time location and time APIs automatically shift the atmosphere between Day and Night modes based on when a user visits. This was a fun dive into rapid prototyping, AI-assisted coding, and playful micro-interactions.
