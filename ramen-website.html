Instant Ramen Website

For my Interaction Foundations class at WashU, I designed and built a responsive website exploring the history, ingredients, production, and iconic brands of instant noodles. Working within a creative director-designer workflow, I translated feedback into a structured web architecture and explored two distinct visual design directions to refine layout, hierarchy, and narrative flow. The final concept was coded from scratch in HTML/CSS, ensuring responsive breakpoints across devices.
