Constellation Digital Ads

During my 10-week creative internship at Constellation, I got to dive headfirst into the fast-paced world of digital advertising for two different spaces: automotive brands and luxury fragrance. Working in Adobe Photoshop and After Effects, I produced and animated over 550 ads for 45+ auto clients across social, video, programmatic, and performance max formats. I also created social-first press ads for Henry Rose, a luxury fragrance brand, by translating long-form editorial quotes into concise, high-impact creatives. This experience taught me how to balance creativity with strict legal compliance, manage high-volume production under tight deadlines, and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to deliver polished ads at scale.
