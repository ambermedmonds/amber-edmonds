WashU Women's Tennis Instagram

As Social Media Manager for the WashU Women's Tennis Instagram, I built a cohesive visual system to elevate the team's social media across match days, score recaps, rankings, and athlete spotlights over two seasons of competition. To manage fast-paced in-season updates, I developed a suite of modular Canva templates for rapid asset creation without sacrificing quality. This streamlined team communications and established a polished, recognizable brand identity for followers.
